title: Safeguards
description: How Assay publishes information about real companies' assets responsibly, and what it never does.
---

Assay publishes information about real companies' assets, so its safeguards
are part of the product, not an afterthought.

## Facts, not ratings

The board shows what was reported, when, by whom, with source links and
timestamps. No grades, rankings or opinions, and no colours that suggest a
judgment. Missing information shows as **unknown**; Assay never estimates a
figure.

## Right of reply

Every asset page has an issuer correction link. Corrections are recorded in
a public [log](/project/corrections/) with their date. A correction form is
planned for T1.

## Legal review

Board wording and disclaimers are reviewed by a lawyer before mainnet
(planned for T3).

## Clear limits

Every page states that Assay is not an audit and not investment advice.
Assay can't detect an attestor who reports false but consistent figures; it
shows disagreement between attestors and supply mismatches instead. See
[Limits](/understand/limits/).

## No funds, no user data

- Assay holds no funds. The guard never moves funds or holds tokens.
- The board uses only public ledger data and public `stellar.toml` files.
- No analytics, cookies or accounts, on the board or on these docs.
