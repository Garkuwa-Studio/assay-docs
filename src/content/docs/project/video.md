A 7-minute narrated walkthrough, recorded on 14 October 2026. It shows:

1. The problem, with the census figures.
2. The board at [assay.wtf](https://assay.wtf/board/): filters, an asset
   page, the method.
3. Cloning, building and testing the core repo.
4. Creating testnet identities.
5. The [registry demo](/build/registry-demo/) and its transactions.
6. The [circuit breaker](/build/circuit-breaker-demo/): two borrows rejected
   on chain, undercovered and stale.
7. Signing a reserve report, verifying it and watching a changed figure fail.
8. The `is_healthy` integration and the guard's reasons.
9. Checking every link and hash, and the dependency scans.
